push notifications when a video goes live) over the same API.

**App name:** MoneyPrinter Autopilot — AI Channel Manager

**Subtitle / short description (80 chars):**
AI creates & posts short videos for your channel daily. You watch it grow.

**Full description:**

> MoneyPrinter Autopilot runs faceless TikTok and Instagram channels for

> ▶ Analytics: views, growth, best-performing topics
> ▶ Built on the open-source engine trusted by 37,000+ developers
>
> Manage everything from your phone. Get notified the moment a video is
> published. Pause, reschedule or redirect a channel in two taps.

**Keywords (App Store, 100 chars):**
`faceless,video,automation,tiktok,shorts,ai video,channel,passive,reels,creator,auto post,growth`

**Category:** Productivity (primary), Photo & Video (secondary)

**Screenshots checklist (both stores):**
1. Channel dashboard with growth curve

- Subscriptions must use native IAP on iOS for app-purchased plans.
