# Marketing campaign: MoneyPrinter Autopilot launch

90-day go-to-market plan. Budget variants: **$0 (organic only)** and
**$3k/mo paid boost**. All copy referenced here is ready in
`docs/store-listing.md`.

## Positioning

- **Category:** channel automation (not "video editor", not "AI video
  generator" — those are red oceans).
- **One-liner:** *Faceless video channels that run themselves.*
- **Audience (in priority order):**
  1. Side-hustlers searching "faceless youtube/tiktok automation" (high
     intent, huge search volume)
  2. Solo marketers/SMMs running client accounts
  3. Agencies (white-label tier)
- **Core proof asset:** our own Autopilot-run channels, public, with
  follower counts visible. The product demonstrates itself in public every
  single day — this is the unfair advantage; everything below leans on it.

## The flywheel (the actual strategy)

```
Autopilot runs demo channels → channels grow in public →
bio links to the tool → users' videos carry subtle watermark →
their viewers discover the tool → more users → more public proof
```

Every other tactic exists to accelerate this loop.

## Phase 0 — Foundation (weeks 1–2)

- [ ] Spin up **3 demo channels** in contrasting niches (e.g. stoicism /
      space facts / money tips) via the Autopilot API; post 2×/day.
- [ ] Landing page: hero = live embed of the demo channels' latest posts +
      follower counters. CTA: "Start your channel in one sentence."
      Capture emails with a free "50 proven faceless niches" PDF.
- [ ] Set up analytics (Plausible), referral tracking, Discord server.
- [ ] Publish the repo's Autopilot docs prominently — open source is the
      top-of-funnel.

## Phase 1 — Launch (weeks 3–4)

- **Product Hunt** launch Tuesday 00:01 PT (copy ready). Rally GitHub
  stargazers + Discord. Goal: top-5 of the day.
- **Hacker News** "Show HN: I built an autopilot for faceless video
  channels (open source)" — lead with engineering (topic-dedup engine,
  scheduling architecture), not money.
- **Reddit**: value-first posts (not ads) in r/SideProject, r/passive_income,
  r/NewTubers, r/Entrepreneur — share the 90-day public experiment:
  "I'm growing 3 fully-automated channels in public, here's week 1 data."
- **X/Twitter + LinkedIn build-in-public thread**, weekly stats updates.

## Phase 2 — Content engine (weeks 5–12, ongoing)

SEO (compounding, the long-term moat) — one post/week targeting:

| Keyword cluster                         | Asset                                   |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| "faceless youtube channel ideas"        | The 50-niches PDF as a long-form post    |
| "tiktok automation tools"               | Honest comparison incl. competitors      |
| "how often to post on tiktok"           | Data from our own demo channels          |
| "moneyprinterturbo tutorial"            | Capture existing OSS search demand       |

YouTube (2 videos/mo): "I let AI run a TikTok channel for 30 days —
results", screen-recorded setup tutorials. These videos historically drive
the highest-converting traffic in this niche.

Email: weekly "Autopilot Operator" newsletter — demo-channel stats, one
niche idea, one tactic. Builds the list into a launch weapon.

## Phase 3 — Paid + partnerships (weeks 8–12, if budget)

$3k/mo split: 50% TikTok/IG ads (creative = literally our product's output,
near-zero production cost, A/B topics weekly), 30% YouTube sponsorships of
small "side hustle" channels (50–200k subs, ~$20–40 CPM), 20% Google Ads on
"faceless channel" exact-match keywords.

**Affiliate program (week 8):** 30% recurring for 12 months via the
referral tracking from Phase 0. The side-hustle audience *is* the affiliate
audience — expect 30–40% of revenue through this channel at maturity (it's
the dominant growth channel for comparable tools).

## Measurement

| Metric                     | Week 4 target | Week 12 target |
| -------------------------- | ------------- | -------------- |
| Demo channels' followers   | 5k combined   | 50k combined   |
| Email list                 | 1,000         | 8,000          |
| Free (self-host) installs  | 500           | 4,000          |
| Paying customers           | 100           | 400            |
| MRR                        | $4.5k         | $18k           |

Kill/iterate rule: any tactic that hasn't produced a paying customer by its
third week gets its budget moved to the best performer.

## Compliance guardrails

- Never make income guarantees in ads or store copy (FTC + ad-platform
  policy; also App Store rejection grounds).
- Disclose AI generation where platforms require it; respect automated
  posting ToS via official APIs only.
- "Results not typical" disclaimer on any case-study numbers.
